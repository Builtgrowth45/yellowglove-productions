What makes Yellow Glove Productions different?

Our focus is on the WHY. Why do you need video? Why is it important to capture this? We combine creativity, strategic thinking, and personalised service. We are creative partners, not order-takers.

What services do you offer?

We specialise in creative video marketing from concept through distribution. Services include video production, animation, motion graphics, photography, 360 tours, and in-house production consultation.

What types of videos can you create?

Brand adverts, corporate videos, event coverage, e-commerce videos, music videos, documentaries, video series, property videos, podcasts, webinars, animations, and more.

How long does a project take?

Typically 4 to 8 weeks from concept to final delivery. We are experienced at working to tight turnarounds - just tell us your deadline upfront.

What does it cost?

Cost depends on video type, length, complexity, and requirements. Every project is different so we build bespoke quotes. Get in touch and we will give you a clear picture.

Can the video be used across multiple platforms?

Absolutely. We design content with versatility in mind. One shoot can yield a hero film, social cuts, short ads, and platform-specific versions.

Do you offer long-term partnerships?

Yes. We offer long-term partnerships and retainer packages for businesses that need ongoing content.

Where are you based and how far do you travel?

Based at LCB Depot in Leicester City Centre. We film across the UK and have shot in Ireland, Portugal, Denmark, and France.

How do I get started?

Just get in touch for a free consultation. We will discuss your goals and figure out what will deliver the best results.

Do you offer in-house production consultation?

Yes. If you want to build internal video capabilities, we can advise on equipment, process, scripting, and editing.
