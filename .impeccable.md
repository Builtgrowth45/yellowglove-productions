## Design Context

### Users
Primarily marketing managers, brand owners, and agency directors in the UK — aged 28-50, budgets from £1k to £30k+. They are evaluating multiple production companies. They visit on a laptop during work hours. They need to feel confident that YGP understands their brand and can execute at a professional level. The job: move from "browsing options" to "booking a call."

### Brand Personality
**Bold · Cinematic · Unapologetic**

YGP does not apologise for its style. The brand is direct, energetic, and proud of its craft. It is not a corporate agency — it is a creative force. The tone is confident without being arrogant. The yellow glove logo signals creativity and irreverence. Think: a music video director who also shoots boardroom testimonials — equally at home in both worlds.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark. Developers use dark interfaces at night. Brand directors use dark interfaces at pitch meetings. The dark palette makes the gold/amber accents pop and references cinematic colour grading.
- **Visual references**: authkit.com (interactive cards, dark cinematic), Linear.app (magnetic cursor, refined motion), Vercel (clean sections, sharp typography)
- **Anti-references**: Generic "video production" Squarespace templates. Glassmorphism. Teal/purple gradients. Any site that could belong to a competitor by swapping the logo.
- **Color**: #FBB038 amber as the single accent. #0a0a0a near-black. #1A1A2E navy as a mid-tone surface. White used sparingly.
- **Type**: Bebas Neue for all display/headings (condensed, bold, cinematic). Barlow Condensed for labels and eyebrows. Poppins 300 for body copy.
- **Motion philosophy**: One high-impact entrance per page section. GSAP ScrollTrigger reveals. Magnetic cursor on interactive elements. Page transitions with yellow curtain wipe.

### Design Principles
1. **The work sells** — portfolio images are the hero, not copy. Let the photography and video speak first.
2. **Earn the scroll** — each section must give something new: a stat, a proof, a story beat. Never repeat.
3. **Yellow is sacred** — #FBB038 appears only on the most important things: the logo, primary CTAs, accent headings, and the glove cursor. Overuse kills its power.
4. **Cinematic spacing** — sections breathe. Generous vertical padding (80–120px). Tight internal groupings. Rhythm varies: tight · generous · tight.
5. **No containers for containers' sake** — if a card doesn't need a box, remove the box. Let type and space do the work.
